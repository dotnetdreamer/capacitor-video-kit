# Video editor fonts - licences

These fonts are bundled with the app so the video editor's text preview and the rendered video use
the same font files, online or offline. Every file is an unmodified WOFF2 subset (latin and
latin-ext) served by the Google Fonts CSS2 API. They are declared in
`src/theme/video-editor-fonts.scss` under "VE "-prefixed family names, which are internal
identifiers and not a renaming of the fonts.

Fifteen families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and one (Special Elite) under the
Apache License 2.0. Both licence texts are reproduced in full at the end of this file.
